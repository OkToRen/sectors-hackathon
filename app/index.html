Read this before the numbers

The roster and sectors are today's. Historical results would describe today's members, not a historical $index portfolio.

Neutral bands are calibrated across today's members rather than on historical data, so a state compares a company with today's LQ45, not with its own past. No historical evidence exists yet, so every evidence label is Insufficient. Unclassified describes missing inputs, not the company.
