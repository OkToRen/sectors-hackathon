Methodology

How states are assigned, and what they cannot tell you

Retrospective research using available historical records; original availability and revisions are not fully verified.

A Company State describes a dated combination of fundamentals, price and foreign flow. It is a prompt to read a financial report, not a forecast.

Publication and vintage limits

Sectors returns the report period, not the date a report became public, and it does not say whether a value was later restated. Each report is treated as public three calendar months after its period end, at the month end. That date is an estimate, labelled as such everywhere it appears, not a regulatory deadline.

Current-universe selection

The roster and the sector of each company are today's. $dates. Any historical result would describe today's members, not a portfolio that held the $index at the time.

Accounting and return conventions

Fundamentals compare the latest standalone quarter's earnings with the same quarter a year earlier, only when both are profits and the basis check passed. Sectors converts members reporting in US dollars to rupiah at one rate per quarter. Dividing each member's rupiah quarters by its published dollar quarters recovers that rate; when every compared quarter is within 1% of the rate shared across reporters, growth is measured in dollars at those rates, so it excludes exchange-rate moves. Members whose figures do not line up stay Unclassified.

Price is the company's $price_days-close price return less the median return of at least $min_peers other members of its sector over the same dates. When fewer than $min_peers sector members have usable closes on those dates, the median of every other $index member with usable closes is used instead, and the company's page names that reference. In this snapshot $roster_reference_count of $member_count members are measured this way. A Pattern Update keeps the reference recorded with the state and never switches it. Closes are unadjusted, so dividends are not included and a corporate action in the window blocks the measure. Foreign flow is net foreign buying and selling over $flow_days trading days, divided by market cap.

Neutral bands

Each dimension has a neutral band. Its half-width is the 25th percentile of absolute values across today's members, calculated from inputs only and never from later returns. This compares a company with today's $index, not with its own past.

Pattern Update

Pattern Update compares today's price and foreign flow with their values when the state was assigned, under the bands recorded with that assignment. Each value is measured as its distance inside the range the state allows, in units of the band. A change of no more than $tolerance of a band counts as steady.

Labels apply in order. Contradicting means a dimension has left its allowed range. Stable means both held steady. Strengthening means both moved further in. Weakening means at least one moved back and neither moved further in. Mixed covers the rest. Neutral shows raw changes only.

The Radar lists the newest observations first. Within a date, larger departures from the assigned pattern come first: Contradicting, then Weakening, Mixed, Strengthening and Stable, then companies without a label, then ticker order. The order is a reading queue, not a ranking of companies.

Split and freeze

$freeze_text Definitions and bands cannot change without a signal log entry and a new freeze.

No chronological training, development and holdout split exists yet, because funded history does not reach far enough back. Bands are calibrated on the current snapshot until it does. When history is funded, bands will be recalibrated on a training period and frozen before any holdout is opened.

Completed outcomes

The outcome is the 60-trading-day return relative to the sector, counted only once the full window has passed. No holdout has been evaluated, so no completed outcome is reported and all $insufficient_count members show Insufficient evidence. At least $min_cases completed holdout cases per state are needed before any outcome is shown.

Reference comparisons

Three simple references will sit beside state results: 60-day sector-relative momentum, comparable-quarter earnings growth, and an equal-weight composite of standardized fundamental, price and foreign-flow measures. They need the same holdout, so none is reported yet. States are not required to beat them.

Uncertainty

With no completed holdout cases there is nothing to estimate, so no rate, interval or significance test is reported. When cases exist, they will overlap in time and share companies and sectors, so they are not independent. Any confidence interval shown then will state that assumption and be labelled optimistic.
