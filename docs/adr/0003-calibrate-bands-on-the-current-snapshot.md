# Calibrate neutral bands on the current snapshot until history is funded

v5 calibrates each dimension's neutral band on chronological training data and keeps a final holdout untouched. On 2026-10-06 the confirmed 500-credit allocation had about 130 credits left, and history costs about 12 credits per ticker-year because prices, foreign flow and quarterly financials are all limited to 90 days or charged per quarter. The operator chose to build with what is affordable now and to add credits later from a teammate's key.

Until then, each band's half-width is the 25th percentile of absolute values across the current LQ45 snapshot, computed from inputs only and never from forward returns. This makes the bands cross-sectional rather than historical: they describe how unusual a company is among today's members, not against its own past. No chronological split or holdout exists yet, so every historical-evidence label stays Insufficient and the reference comparisons are recorded as unavailable. The Methodology view states this deviation plainly. When history is funded, the bands are recalibrated on a training period and frozen before any holdout is opened, as v5 requires; states assigned under this ADR are then superseded, not mixed with the historical ones.
